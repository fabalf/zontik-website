How it works

One partner from your loading dock to your customer's door. Each step is planned around your products and the market you start in.

  1. 1

    Assess

    We pick the products and the first country with the best odds, and model your landed cost and margin.

  2. 2

    Prepare

    Product registrations, labeling, localized listings in Spanish, and U.S. sourcing where it helps you qualify for lower duties.

  3. 3

    Ship

    We choose the route and mode that serve each shipment best, and handle freight and customs into marketplace warehouses.

  4. 4

    Sell

    We run your store: pricing in local currency, inventory, advertising and customer service.

  5. 5

    Grow

    Monthly reporting, then expansion to new countries and marketplaces once the first market works.

Two ways to work with us

Choose how much control you want to keep, and how much of the work and risk you want us to carry.

Managed export

You keep ownership of your goods and your marketplace stores. We set up and run everything for you.

  • Your brand, your accounts, your inventory
  • Setup fees, a monthly fee and a share of sales
  • Best for brands that want to build their own presence abroad

We buy, we sell

We purchase your products in the United States and resell them in Latin America under our own accounts.

  • You receive a U.S. purchase order, paid in dollars
  • No foreign registrations, currency risk or customs paperwork for you
  • Best for first-time exporters who want to test demand
